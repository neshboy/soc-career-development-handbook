A SOC Career Development Handbook. Built for the analyst. Free. Public.

A certification is not a career plan. Neither is a title. The climb is everything you build in between.

From self-assessment → study plan → home lab → portfolio → interview prep → the branch point → team lead → SOC manager → SOC architect.

Inside: surviving L1 and earning a fast L2 nomination, closing the judgment gap, the senior-analyst portfolio that earns a branch, work-sample bars for detection engineer, threat hunter, incident responder, and staff/principal IC, certification sequencing checked directly against CompTIA, GIAC, ISC2, and OffSec's own current pages — not pattern-guessed, and burnout, plateau, and pacing self-diagnosis across a full career, not just a first year.

Every stage is worked from the seat you're actually sitting in — L1, L2, L3, branch specialist, team lead, SOC manager, and SOC architect — cross-referenced against the competency matrix and promotion committee it's actually scored by, never re-deriving either.
